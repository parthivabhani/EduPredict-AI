  • Class Weighting: balanced (mitigates 84:16 class imbalance)

  • Outcome: Achieved top overall ROC-AUC of 0.9605 and 91.54% Accuracy.

Research Hypothesis, Theoretical Foundations & Literature Review

Research Hypotheses & Statistical Confirmation

Empirically Validated

Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$) [Accepted]:

"Students' demographic, academic, and behavioral attributes have a statistically significant relationship with their final academic performance ($G3$). Machine learning algorithms can accurately predict student performance and assist educational institutions in identifying students who require academic support."

Null Hypothesis ($H_0$) [Rejected]:

"Students' demographic, academic, and behavioral attributes have no statistically significant relationship with their final academic performance, and machine learning models cannot reliably predict outcomes."
